Zenith: a 5v5 round-based tactical FPS at a mountaintop observatory under an aurora sky, built on three.js. Two teams of five fight short rounds — plant the spike at Dome or Dish, or eliminate the other team — with money earned each round, a buy menu, four guns, HE, smoke and flashbang grenades, and bots that run a called plan (three push the site, two lurk the fake), hold angles, peek from cover, fall back when hurt, react to gunfire, smoke the site as they move in to plant, and call it all out on the radio. First to 7 rounds takes the match, and sides swap at halftime, so both halves of the spike game have to be played. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make the best three.js tactical FPS" bounty, $1,000 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — click to play. WASD to move, mouse to look, click to fire, R to reload, B to buy, G for a grenade, H for smoke, F for a flashbang, E to plant or defuse the spike. First to 7 rounds takes the match.
